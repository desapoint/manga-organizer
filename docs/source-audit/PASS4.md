## Critical design conflicts and decisions still open

- Maki directly deletes a reviewed physical duplicate and completes DB cleanup without cancellation; Manga Organizer should default to quarantine plus verified survivor and recovery, not copy the permanent-delete workflow.
- Uchiyomi two-phase numbering handles cycles and rollback, but its preflight target existence check does not prove atomic no-clobber under external writers. Require platform-safe publication, journal and post-crash reconciliation.
- Manga-arr stages, rechecks lease, writes a prepared barrier, then publishes; use explicit reversible/irreversible phases. Its full multi-thousand-line recovery machinery is not qualified for our target mounts by this inspection.
- Kavita's watcher validation cap can eventually scan without waiting for validation; inventory scan and physical mutation must have separate admission criteria.
- Mihon duplicate SQL uses title substring OR matching nonzero tracker IDs, not title only. Bibliographic matching is a suggestion; never treat as a duplicate file.
- Maki and Uchiyomi page retrieval vs Suwayomi-managed queue need explicit ownership, no two competing schedulers. SW branch and release differ in auth annotations and method signatures; introspection plus authenticated fixture tests required.
